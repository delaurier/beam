Everything a flow changes is saved in the browser, under the key `beam-farm-v1`. There is no server yet. Clearing that key puts the sample farm back.

## Add a sale

1. On [Corn marketing](/app/crops/corn/marketing), press **Add sale**. From any other screen, use **Add** in the top bar and choose Sale.
2. Enter the date, bushels and futures price. Basis and premium are optional and count as zero.
3. Press **Add sale**. A mistake keeps the form open and says what to fix.
4. The sale joins the table, newest first. Sold, unsold, average price, margin and revenue update, and so does the Overview.

## Change or remove a row

1. Press **Edit** on the row, double-click it, or focus it and press Enter.
2. Change the values. Computed cells update as you type.
3. Press Enter or the tick to save, Escape or the cross to cancel.
4. **Delete** sits under the open row. It asks once.

The same steps work for expenses, fields and equipment.

On [Reports](/app/reports), **CSV** downloads the report from the data as it is now.
